Pliant

My role has focused on building scalable underwriting, portfolio-management and risk-analytics capabilities while also establishing the foundations of the business's data-science function.

Credit strategy and underwriting approach

Model validation

Portfolio performance

Default outcomes

Risk controls and monitoring

Credit Decision Engine

Problem

Manual underwriting limited the number of onboarding cases the credit team could process efficiently and created unnecessary variation in turnaround times.

Solution

I designed and built a credit decision engine that combines policy rules, data inputs and automated recommendations to determine which applications can be processed automatically and which require human review.

Business impact

The engine now automates approximately 50% of onboarding cases.

This has:

Increased underwriting capacity

Improved throughput

Reduced the amount of analyst time spent on straightforward cases

Allowed underwriters to focus on more complex or higher-risk applications

Supported faster and more consistent credit decisions

Why it mattered

The objective was not simply to automate decisions. The engine was designed to create a scalable underwriting process where automation handles cases that can be decided confidently and analysts remain focused on situations where judgement adds the most value.

AI-Assisted Underwriting and Credit Memo Pre-Population

Problem

Credit underwriters spend significant time gathering background information and assembling evidence before they can begin the actual credit assessment.

Solution

I built AI-enabled tools that gather relevant background research and evaluate available credit information before pre-populating the credit memo.

The objective is to give the underwriter a structured, evidence-based starting point rather than a blank case.

Business impact

The tools reduce repetitive case preparation and allow underwriters to spend more time on judgement, challenge and exception handling.

They also improve consistency by ensuring that relevant information is brought into the assessment in a repeatable format.

Financial Statement OCR and Spreading

Problem

Company financial statements contain important underwriting information, but manually extracting and spreading the relevant values is time consuming.

Solution

I developed an AI/OCR workflow that extracts relevant financial information from company accounts and converts it into structured data for use in the credit process.

Business impact

The workflow creates the foundation for reducing manual financial spreading and increasing the proportion of the underwriting process that can be automated.

It also allows extracted data to feed directly into downstream credit assessment and decisioning rather than remaining trapped in unstructured documents.

Continuous Credit Monitoring and Early-Warning Indicators

Problem

Credit risk changes after onboarding. A point-in-time underwriting decision is therefore not sufficient for managing an active portfolio.

Solution

I implemented automated continuous monitoring across the customer portfolio using multiple data sources.

The monitoring incorporates:

Credit-agency data

Open-banking transaction data

Customer liquidity / runway indicators

Adverse-media signals

Other emerging credit-risk indicators

Automated alerts are generated when predefined risk thresholds are breached.

Business impact

The capability moved portfolio management toward proactive exception-based monitoring rather than relying solely on periodic manual reviews.

This gives the risk team earlier visibility of deteriorating customers and allows analysts to focus attention where new information indicates that the original risk assessment may no longer be valid.

Customer Liquidity and Runway Monitoring

Problem

For commercial customers, deterioration in liquidity can be an important early signal of increased credit risk.

Solution

I updated the runway simulation used to monitor customer financial health using open-banking transactional data.

The methodology uses observed transaction behaviour to assess available liquidity and estimate customer runway.

Business impact

The resulting indicators feed into ongoing portfolio monitoring and trigger alerts where customer liquidity falls below defined thresholds.

This gives the credit team a more dynamic view of financial health than relying only on historic financial statements.

Adverse-Media Monitoring

Solution

As part of the broader early-warning framework, I developed an AI-enabled adverse-media monitoring process connected to multiple data sources.

The process searches for emerging negative information relating to customers and relevant individuals and highlights results that may require credit review.

Business impact

This expands the risk team's monitoring coverage and reduces reliance on manual searches, particularly where relevant information appears outside standard credit-agency data.

Expected Credit Loss Methodology

Problem

The business required a methodology for assessing expected credit loss and determining appropriate provisioning at individual-exposure level.

Solution

I designed an individual-exposure ECL methodology that links the assessment of customer credit risk with provisioning requirements.

Business impact

The methodology created a more structured and consistent approach to assessing expected losses across individual exposures and strengthened the connection between portfolio risk assessment and financial provisioning.

Insurance API and Credit Insurance Strategy

Problem

Credit insurance can reduce exposure on eligible customers, but incorporating coverage into underwriting requires both operational integration and a clear decision strategy.

Solution

I implemented an API connection to the insurance provider so that coverage could be requested during onboarding.

Alongside the technical integration, I created a credit strategy defining how insurance availability should influence the underwriting process.

Business impact

This embedded insurance directly into the credit-decision workflow rather than treating it as a separate manual process.

It allowed the underwriting strategy to account for external risk mitigation at the point a customer was being assessed.

Jira Underwriting Workflow Integration

Problem

Even where risk analysis is automated, underwriters still need the relevant information and recommendation presented clearly in the operational tool where the case is actually managed.

Solution

I implemented an integration with Jira, the credit team's case-management tool.

Case tickets were automatically populated with relevant application and risk information.

Where the case could be automated, the ticket could include a recommendation. Where manual review was required, the underwriter began with much of the relevant case information already assembled.

Business impact

The integration reduced manual case setup, connected analytical outputs to the team's day-to-day workflow and helped ensure that automation translated into actual operational efficiency rather than existing as a separate analytical process.
